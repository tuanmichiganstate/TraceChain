# Product modes Phase 8: pilot and calibration

## Outcome

Phase 8 has completed its repository-controlled technical preparation against
the immutable Phase 7 application commit. Participant data collection has not
started because the protocol approvals and measurement thresholds remain
unrecorded.

This is the intended boundary. Phase 8 is an empirical validation phase, not
another application-feature phase.

## Completed repository work

- The pilot and validation protocol defines participants, learning sequence,
  measures, privacy rules, accessibility review, and exit criteria.
- The Phase 8 instrument pack supplies parallel concept-discrimination forms,
  Audit observations, critical usability tasks, rubric-reliability records,
  assistive-technology review, and Vietnamese content review.
- Assignment, Audit, competency, counterfactual, process, and variant reports
  retain exact content and replay identities.
- Audit class reporting exposes finding sensitivity inputs, false positives,
  missed findings, evidence and policy citations, severity, score, timing,
  variant distribution, and review-only calibration.
- The technical baseline records the exact source, CI, browser, package,
  hosted, and Moodle evidence.
- The pilot execution record keeps automated readiness separate from human
  evidence.

No second analytics engine, pilot score, automatic score rescaling, or
participant-data store was added.

## Data-collection stop condition

Data collection remains unauthorized while any required protocol approval is
pending or while the threshold table is blank. The repository must not invent
approvers, sample sizes, educational-validity thresholds, participant results,
or acceptance decisions.

Once approvals exist, the operator should:

1. Freeze the exact platform and instrument identities.
2. Store consent and identity linkage outside TraceChain.
3. Run the approved learner, instructor, author, accessibility, and content
   review sessions.
4. Export assignment-scoped pseudonymous evidence.
5. Review results by exact scenario and variant before aggregating.
6. Record rubric agreement using the approved criterion-level method.
7. Resolve critical accessibility and content findings.
8. Link every exit decision to evidence in the execution record.
9. Re-rank later product work only after the product-review authority accepts
   the evidence.

## Deliberate limits

- Technical checks do not establish learning effectiveness.
- Automated accessibility does not establish screen-reader usability.
- Similar mean scores do not establish variant equivalence.
- Pseudonymous records are not anonymous.
- Counterfactual results remain exploratory and do not replace grades.
- Audit Assessment remains a calibration candidate.
- No collaboration, AI grading, new laboratory, or new score was added.

The product-modes roadmap has no further implementation phase after Phase 8.
Later initiatives require a new evidence-based product decision.
