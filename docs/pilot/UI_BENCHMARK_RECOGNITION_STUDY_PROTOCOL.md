# SimuLedger UI Benchmark Recognition Study Protocol

## Status

Protocol fixed before participant review. Human sessions have not yet been run.

## Purpose

Test whether the six benchmark surfaces communicate role, task, system layer,
and transaction state without coaching. This is a recognition check, not a
preference survey.

## Participants

Recruit six participants:

- Three learners, including at least one person new to blockchain
- Two lecturers who could assign or debrief SimuLedger
- One participant who regularly uses keyboard navigation, screen
  magnification, or a screen reader

Do not change the pass thresholds after reviewing the results.

## Surfaces

Show the following without explanatory captions:

1. Stage 3 Certificate Verification Console
2. Stage 5 Receiving and Discrepancy Management
3. Stage 8 Blockchain Verification Laboratory
4. Stage 9 Recall Command Center
5. Ledger and transaction explorer
6. Stage 4 mobile logistics handoff at 320 px

Include proposed, pending, rejected, committed, and completed states where the
surface supports them.

## Questions

For each surface, ask the participant to identify:

1. Their current role
2. Their current professional task
3. Which content is instruction and which is an operational application
4. Which content is business state and which is ledger history
5. Which control is a professional decision and which is a knowledge checkpoint
6. Whether the visible transaction is proposed, rejected, pending, or committed

Record the response, time to first confident answer, hesitation, and any
incorrect interpretation. Do not teach the visual vocabulary until the
participant has completed all six surfaces.

## Predefined pass rule

The benchmark passes only when:

- At least five of six participants identify the current role and professional
  task on at least five of the six surfaces.
- At least four of six distinguish professional decisions from knowledge
  checkpoints on every applicable surface.
- At least four of six distinguish business evidence from blockchain evidence
  on Stages 3, 8, and 9.
- At least four of six identify the transaction state correctly in every tested
  proposed, rejected, pending, and committed example.
- No participant encounters a blocker that prevents keyboard, zoom, or
  screen-reader use.

A serious accessibility blocker fails the benchmark regardless of the aggregate
recognition counts.
